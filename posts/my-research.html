Research Overview

My research focuses on Brain Image Segmentation using Hyperspectral Datasets. Image segmentation is a critical task in computer vision where an image is divided into meaningful regions for analysis.

In medical applications, segmentation helps identify important anatomical structures, tissues, and abnormalities. Accurate segmentation plays a significant role in medical diagnosis, treatment planning, and research.

Every breakthrough begins with a question that nobody has answered yet.

Why Hyperspectral Imaging?

Traditional RGB images contain only three color channels. Hyperspectral images, however, capture information across dozens or even hundreds of spectral bands.

Each pixel contains detailed spectral information that can reveal characteristics invisible to conventional imaging systems.

This rich information makes hyperspectral imaging highly valuable in medical research, where small differences between tissues may carry important clinical meaning.
