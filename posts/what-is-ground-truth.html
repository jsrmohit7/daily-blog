What is Ground Truth?

Understanding the reference labels that make image segmentation possible.

While studying image segmentation and deep learning, I frequently encountered the term Ground Truth. It is one of the most fundamental concepts in machine learning because every model needs a correct answer to learn from.

What is Ground Truth?

Ground Truth refers to the actual and verified labels used as a reference during training and evaluation. In image segmentation, Ground Truth usually takes the form of masks created manually by experts.

These masks tell the model which pixels belong to which class, allowing it to learn the correct segmentation boundaries.

Ground Truth in Brain Image Segmentation

Suppose we have a brain image containing tumor tissue. An expert manually outlines the tumor region pixel by pixel. This manually created mask becomes the Ground Truth.

When a segmentation model makes predictions, its output is compared against this Ground Truth mask to determine how accurate the prediction is.

Why is Ground Truth Important?

  • Provides correct labels for training.
  • Acts as the benchmark for evaluation.
  • Allows calculation of metrics like Dice Score and IoU.
  • Helps compare different segmentation models.
  • Ensures that predictions can be objectively measured.

Ground Truth in My Research

During my research internship at the QUEDS Department, BIT Mesra, I received Ground Truth masks corresponding to hyperspectral brain images. These masks represent the true segmentation and will serve as the reference for future experiments involving image segmentation models.

Without Ground Truth, training supervised models such as U-Net becomes extremely difficult because the network would have no target labels to learn from.

Challenges in Creating Ground Truth

  • Manual annotation is time-consuming.
  • Requires domain experts.
  • Different experts may produce slightly different masks.
  • Large datasets are difficult to annotate completely.

Ground Truth and Evaluation Metrics

Metrics such as Dice Similarity Coefficient and Intersection over Union (IoU) compare the predicted segmentation mask with the Ground Truth mask. Higher overlap indicates better segmentation performance.

A model can only learn what the Ground Truth teaches it.

Key Takeaway

Ground Truth is the reference answer in machine learning and image segmentation. It provides the correct labels that enable models to learn, evaluate, and improve their predictions.
