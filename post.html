Building Consistency

Small actions repeated every day eventually become remarkable.

Most people overestimate what they can achieve in a week and underestimate what they can achieve in a year.

Why Consistency Wins

Motivation comes and goes. Systems remain. A simple habit repeated every day creates momentum that compounds over time.

The goal is not perfection. The goal is showing up even when the excitement disappears.

Success is rarely dramatic. It is usually the result of small actions repeated consistently.

Daily Practice

Write one page. Read ten pages. Walk for thirty minutes. Build one feature.

The habit matters more than intensity.

Final Thought

Consistency is the closest thing to a superpower. Start small and stay patient.
